Research

My current project investigates branching algorithms and backdoor variables for combinatorial optimization problems. It has been observed empirically that there exists a small subset of variables for which if the optimization solver only branches on them, the resulting subproblem is easy and the search can be completed extremely efficiently. Finding these backdoor variables, however, is NP-hard, and I am interested in finding these backdoors through vertex enumeration, sampling techniques and generative models.
